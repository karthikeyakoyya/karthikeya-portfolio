About

I graduated from NIT Durgapur in May 2026 with a B.Tech in Computer Science, but most of what I actually know I learned by building: wiring an ESP32 to a motion sensor at 2am, debugging a geofence that was off by 40 meters, and arguing with a TF-IDF classifier about what counts as "urgent."

All while actively interviewing for full-time roles.

Looking for a team where I can keep doing that: test automation and quality engineering, full-stack work, applied ML, and the occasional excuse to solder something.
